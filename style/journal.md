### 72 Hours Before Liftoff

**Mission Log** · **Starlink** · **Behind the Scenes**

*By the SpaceX Mission Team · 9 min read*

Three days out, the pad goes quiet in a way that only the launch team notices. Every system has already been checked twice, and what's left is the slow, careful work of making sure nothing was missed.

> The countdown doesn't start at zero — it starts the moment the rocket is stacked.

### What happens in the final stretch

The static fire test comes first, a brief ignition while the rocket stays firmly on the pad. Every second of data from that test is reviewed before the team clears the vehicle for flight.

From there, it's weather, range safety, and payload checks — each team reporting "go" in turn during the final poll before fueling begins.

### Flight sequence

1. Propellant load begins
2. Engine chill and pre-valve checks
3. Terminal count auto-sequence starts
4. Main engine ignition and liftoff
